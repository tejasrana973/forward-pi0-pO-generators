# ATLAS p+O data (HEPData)

Tables of the ATLAS measurement of charged-particle production in p+O collisions at 9.62 TeV.

- Record: https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins3140590 (version 1)
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.169575
- License: CC0

| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| Table1.csv | (1/N_ev) dN_ev/dn_ch (Fig. 2a of the paper) |
| Table2.csv | (1/N_ev) (1/2 pi pT) d2N/(deta dpT) (Fig. 2b) |
| Table3.csv | mean pT versus n_ch (Fig. 2c) |
| Table4.csv | (1/N_ev) dN/deta (Fig. 2d) |

The files are copies of the CSV download of the record. Columns: x, x low, x high, y, stat+, stat-, sys+, sys-.
